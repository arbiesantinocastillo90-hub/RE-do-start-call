    HOW TO PLAY READ · REMEMBER · DECIDE

    Watch the amber warning cells. They disappear before your decision; the enemy strikes those exact cells after your move and attack. Move one cardinal tile or HOLD, then choose Jab, Sweep or Burst and select a target. Your attacks leave lingering marks.

    Clear three rooms. After the first two, choose one of three modifiers; each has a benefit and a cost.

    CONTENT NOTICE SYMBOLIC DISTRESS

    References childhood distress, fear, isolation and memories of abuse through non-graphic environments and sounds. There is no literal depiction of abuse. The three-room ending is a demo, not the complete story.
